ELF GIFT TOWER

Grab gifts in the garden tunnel and stack them under your team's tree. First team to the target height wins!
Grab a BOMB 💣 and run into a rival team's tree to blow it up. Taller piles need more bombs.
When a tree falls, its gifts SCATTER around the base for anyone to grab, and that tree gets a 20-second shield.
STEAL from elves on the move: bump a rival elf CARRYING more gifts than you to take their stack. You can't steal from a tree's pile, and bases are safe zones.

Type your elf name below and press JOIN

Only one person (the host) starts the game. Their screen runs it, so they should keep the tab open.

HOST CONTROLS

Move: WASD or arrow keys · Bombs blink orange on the mini-map · Phone/tablet: touch and drag toward where you want to go
Host: pick Teams × Players per team (for example 2 × 2 = 4 elves). Empty spots are filled with computer elves. Auto splits whoever joined into teams of about 6.
